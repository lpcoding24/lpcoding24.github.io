This policy explains what information Coronation Chess collects, why, and how you can control it. Coronation Chess is a chess app offering local pass-and-play, an AI opponent at five difficulty levels, a daily puzzle, a timed Puzzle Rush mode, and optional accounts for live multiplayer, friends, messaging, achievements, and leaderboards.

Information we collect

Account information

Creating an account is optional — local play, the AI opponent, the Daily Puzzle, and Puzzle Rush all work without one. Signing in is required only for live multiplayer, friends, messaging, and having your progress saved to an account rather than just your device. If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password (used for authentication only — we never see or store your password directly; it's handled by our authentication provider, Google Firebase)
  • Username (chosen by you) and avatar selection
  • Optionally, your country (used to show a flag on your profile)
  • Win/loss/draw statistics, broken down by time control
  • Achievement progress, hint balance, Daily Puzzle solve count, and Puzzle Rush best score, once you're signed in — these are saved to your account so they survive a reinstall or a new device, rather than being lost the way purely on-device progress would be
  • An approximate online/offline status, updated periodically while the app is open
Leaderboards are public. If you're signed in, your username, avatar, country (if set), and your scores in whichever leaderboard category you rank in (wins by time control, puzzles solved, Puzzle Rush best) can be seen by other players on the Global leaderboard — not just your friends. You can still use the app, including online play, without appearing on a leaderboard, since ranking only reflects activity you've actually done in that category.

Gameplay data

  • Online game state (moves, clock times, results) while a game is in progress or recently finished
  • If you're signed out, Daily Puzzle streaks, Puzzle Rush scores, achievement progress, and hint balance are stored only on your device, not on our servers

Social features, blocking, and reporting

If you use friends or messaging, we store your friends list, sent and received friend requests, and the messages you send to friends. Messages are only visible to you and the friend you're messaging.

You can block another player at any time — this removes any existing friendship or pending request between you and prevents them from sending you new friend requests, game invites, or messages. Blocking is enforced on our servers, not just hidden in the app, and nobody can see who has blocked them or browse anyone else's block list.

You can also report a user (for harassment, spam, inappropriate content, or other concerns), optionally with a short note. Reports are sent for our review and are not visible to the reported user or to anyone other than us.

Advertising and tracking

Coronation Chess shows ads via Google AdMob to support free use of the app (Pro removes them). On iOS, we ask for permission (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency system) before using your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you'll still see ads, just less personalized ones. AdMob may also collect other device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads regardless of this choice. We don't control what AdMob itself collects — see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases

In-app purchases — the one-time Pro unlock and consumable hint packs — are processed by Apple's App Store and managed through RevenueCat, which tells us what you've purchased so the app can unlock it or add hints to your balance. We never see your payment details — Apple handles those directly. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

Hint pack purchases made while signed in are saved to your account the same way other progress is; purchases made while signed out are only saved to that device.

Notifications

With your permission, Coronation Chess can send a daily local reminder to come back and play. This notification is scheduled entirely on your device — no data about you is sent anywhere to make this happen. You can turn this off anytime in Settings, and can revoke notification permission entirely in your device's system settings.

How we use this information

  • To operate core features: your account, matchmaking, friends, messaging, achievements, and leaderboards
  • To show ads and unlock purchases you've made
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse, including acting on reports

We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and security

Account and gameplay data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Firebase applies industry-standard security practices; see Firebase's Privacy and Security policies for details.

Your choices

  • Most of the app works without an account at all
  • You can remove friends, block other users, and leave conversations at any time from within the app
  • You can delete your account entirely from within the app — go to Profile and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, friends, messages, and stats. This is immediate and doesn't require contacting us, though you're welcome to reach out with any questions first

Children's privacy

Coronation Chess is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
