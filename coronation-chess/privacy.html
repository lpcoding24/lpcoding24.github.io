Coronation Chess — Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Coronation Chess collects, why, and how you can control it. Coronation Chess is a chess app with local play, an AI opponent, a daily puzzle, and optional online accounts for live multiplayer, friends, and messaging.

Information we collect

Account information

Creating an account is optional — local play, the AI opponent, and the daily puzzle all work without one. Signing in is required only for online multiplayer, friends, and messaging. If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and password (used for authentication only — we never see or store your password directly; it's handled by our authentication provider, Google Firebase)
  • Username (chosen by you, visible to other players)
  • Avatar selection and, optionally, your country (used to show a flag on your profile)
  • Win/loss/draw statistics for online games
  • An approximate online/offline status, updated periodically while the app is open

Gameplay data

  • Online game state (moves, clock times, results) while a game is in progress or recently finished
  • Daily puzzle streaks and hint usage are stored only on your device, not on our servers

Social features

If you use friends or messaging, we store your friends list, friend requests, and the messages you send to friends. Messages are only visible to you and the friend you're messaging.

Advertising

Coronation Chess shows ads via Google AdMob to support free use of the app (Pro removes them). AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. We don't control what AdMob collects — see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases

In-app purchases (such as Pro) are processed by Apple's App Store and managed through RevenueCat, which tells us whether you've purchased Pro so the app can unlock it. We never see your payment details — Apple handles those directly. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

How we use this information

  • To operate core features: your account, matchmaking, friends, messaging, and stats
  • To show ads and unlock purchases you've made
  • To keep the app secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and security

Account and gameplay data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Firebase applies industry-standard security practices; see Firebase's Privacy and Security policies for details.

Your choices

  • Most of the app works without an account at all
  • You can remove friends and leave conversations at any time from within the app
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below

Children's privacy

Coronation Chess is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
